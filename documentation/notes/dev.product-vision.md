Kato's north star is durable local ownership of AI conversations: users should be able to capture, organize, review, and reuse their AI work as ordinary files inside boundaries they explicitly control.

The product should stay local-first, provider-agnostic, and security-bounded: the daemon performs the long-running capture work, while the CLI and web console provide operator controls over configuration, status, and output workflows.

Shipped capabilities live in [[features]], prioritized product work lives in [[roadmap]], technical and administrative nits live in [[dev.todo]], and looser future ideas live in [[product-ideas]].
